Platform Terms of Use

By creating or using a ForgeFit account, you agree to these Platform Terms and acknowledge the Privacy Policy and Health Disclaimer. You must be at least 18 years old to create an account during the beta.

ForgeFit’s role

ForgeFit provides technology that helps members discover, communicate with, and organize services with independent fitness and wellness professionals. ForgeFit is not a gym, healthcare provider, employer, agent, insurer, payment processor, or party to the professional–client relationship.

Your account

Provide accurate information, keep your credentials secure, and promptly report unauthorized access. Do not impersonate another person, misuse another member’s information, evade account restrictions, or use ForgeFit unlawfully.

Beta service

The beta is provided without membership charges and may change, pause, or contain defects. We may restrict or terminate access for safety, abuse, legal compliance, or violations of these terms. When paid memberships are introduced, pricing, renewal, cancellation, and refund terms will be presented before purchase.

No guaranteed results

Profiles, credentials, reviews, recommendations, availability, results, and user statements may be incomplete or inaccurate. ForgeFit does not guarantee a professional’s identity, licensing, certification, suitability, availability, performance, or outcomes unless a feature expressly states that a specific verification was completed.

Privacy Policy

ForgeFit may process account and profile details, goals, measurements, body-fat estimates, nutrition and workout records, progress media, location preferences, schedules, messages, service agreements, reviews, marketplace activity, uploaded files, and technical/security logs.

How information is used

  • Provide accounts, dashboards, search, scheduling, messaging, programs, progress tracking, community, and marketplace features.
  • Apply privacy and sharing choices, maintain security, prevent fraud, troubleshoot, and comply with law.
  • Improve ForgeFit using appropriately limited analytics. Marketing communications require a separate choice.

Sharing and visibility

Information is shared according to the feature and your settings—for example, with a selected professional, an accepted service connection, the public community, or marketplace members. Do not upload information you lack permission to share. Precise home addresses should never be posted publicly.

Service providers and legal requests

Vendors such as hosting, database, storage, email, security, and future payment providers may process limited information to operate ForgeFit. Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect safety, or enforce these terms. ForgeFit does not sell personal information during the beta.

Retention, access, and deletion

Information is retained while needed for your account, security, dispute resolution, and legal obligations. Some records may remain in backups or where required by law. Members may request access, correction, export, or deletion by contacting ForgeFit. Privacy rights vary by location.

Security and health information

ForgeFit uses reasonable safeguards, but no online service is risk-free. ForgeFit should not be assumed to be HIPAA-covered or “HIPAA compliant” merely because it stores fitness information. Avoid uploading medical records unless a future feature expressly supports them.

Health, Fitness, and Nutrition Disclaimer

ForgeFit content, calculations, estimates, programs, and communications are for informational and organizational purposes and are not medical diagnosis, treatment, emergency care, or a substitute for advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

Exercise and nutrition changes involve risks. Consult an appropriate clinician before beginning or changing a program, particularly if you are pregnant, injured, taking medication, or have a medical condition. Stop activity and seek appropriate care for pain, faintness, breathing difficulty, or other concerning symptoms. Call emergency services in an emergency.

BMI, BMR, calorie, body-fat, trend, and performance calculations are estimates and may be inaccurate. Do not rely on them for medical decisions.

Independent Professional Services

Professionals are independent providers responsible for their credentials, licenses, insurance, scope of practice, taxes, services, communications, safety procedures, pricing, cancellations, refunds, and compliance with applicable laws. Professionals must not make false credentials, medical claims, or guaranteed-result claims.

A client request does not create a service relationship. The professional sends separate Service Terms; access to direct messaging and the professional wall may begin only after the client affirmatively accepts those terms. Either party may end the connection subject to any lawful obligations in their separate agreement. ForgeFit records the workflow but is not a party to that agreement.

Physical therapy, medical nutrition therapy, telehealth, and other licensed services may require additional verification and compliance before being offered through ForgeFit.

Marketplace Terms

ForgeFit provides member-to-member listings and messaging but does not own, inspect, authenticate, ship, warrant, or process payment for listed items during the beta. Buyers and sellers are responsible for condition, legality, payment, delivery, returns, taxes, and personal safety.

Prohibited listings include illegal, stolen, recalled, counterfeit, unsafe, prescription, controlled, ingestible, or misleadingly described items and any item ForgeFit determines creates unreasonable risk. Meet in a safe public location, inspect items before paying, protect personal information, and report suspicious activity.

Community Guidelines

Gym Culture, walls, reviews, and messages exist to support constructive connection. Do not post harassment, threats, hate, sexual exploitation, unwanted explicit content, dangerous misinformation, spam, scams, doxxing, copyright violations, or content that invades another person’s privacy.

Reviews must reflect genuine experiences. ForgeFit will not require positive sentiment, buy positive reviews, or suppress a review merely because it is negative. Sponsored, affiliate, employee, or other material relationships must be clearly disclosed. Members may report content; ForgeFit may remove content or restrict accounts to protect users and the service.

You retain ownership of content you create. You grant ForgeFit a limited, non-exclusive license to host, process, reproduce, and display it only as needed to operate the features and visibility settings you choose. Public content may be seen, copied, or reshared by others despite our controls.

You must have permission from every identifiable person in uploaded progress photos, videos, testimonials, client results, or documents. A client’s private information may not be used for a professional’s marketing without the client’s separate, informed consent. Deleting content removes it from active display, subject to reasonable processing time, backups, legal retention, and copies others independently made.
